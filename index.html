Well hello there, I'm Dirk.

Game Developer | Gameplay Programmer

I'm Dirk ten Have, currently studying HBO-ICT at the Hogeschool van Amsterdam. I enjoy creating games and building the gameplay systems behind them.

My current main focus is gameplay programming: creating the core mechanics and systems that bring a game to life. I mainly work with Unity, but I've also explored Godot, MonoGame, C++ and other things. I'm always looking to improve and become a more all-round game developer.

I began programming in 2016 during a week long programming bootcamp, where I fell in love with programming for the very first time and have never stopped ever since.
