Well hello there, I'm Dirk.

Game Developer | Gameplay Programmer

My name is Dirk ten Have, I am an current Game Developer Student from the Hogeschool van Amsterdam and I love creating games for the people all around the world.

My main specialty in programming is mainly focused on gameplay programming and creating the core mechanics which makes the core tick. I enjoy working on such problems be solved and be shown visually on screen.

Most of my experiences have been mainly in Unity, however, I've as well experienced programming in many different areas, for example Godot, C#, MonoGame, C++, etc. While gameplay development is currently my strongest interest, I'm constantly trying to become better at my very craft where in the end its my main goal to be an all-round game dev.
